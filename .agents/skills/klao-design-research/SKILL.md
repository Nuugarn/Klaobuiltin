# KLAO Design Research

Provide concise, implementable design guidance for KLAO's portfolio-focused static website. Follow [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) and relevant [PROJECT.md](../../../docs/PROJECT.md) / [WORKFLOW.md](../../../docs/WORKFLOW.md) sections resolved from this repository.

## When and how to use

- Manager assigns a target/outcome and deliverables when research will help an unresolved design choice. Do not commission extra roles, research, or documentation automatically.
- A supplied reference or chosen owner direction skips this skill and alternative proposals. Small UI changes use the executor's delegated design judgment.
- A commissioned specialist is read-only for repository source/assets/docs and Git. Return findings to Manager; the assigned executor implements them.
- There is no independent Reviewer, mandatory three-direction presentation, or separate fidelity approval gate.
- Figma work uses existing authenticated tools, applicable skills, verified access, and scoped rights. Do not change sharing, seats, billing, or permissions without an explicit request.

## 1. Discover only what the task needs

Read the relevant target and actual imagery without dumping embedded payloads. Separate repository facts, owner direction, and assumptions. Ask only about choices that materially change the requested outcome and are outside delegated discretion; reuse settled answers.

Missing audience, service-area, business, or copy details may be inferred or drafted under AGENTS. Identify assumptions for the owner's visual review rather than blocking progress. Do not describe invented details as verified or stock/AI concepts as documentary evidence of completed projects.

## 2. Research only enough to resolve the choice

Inspect a small relevant primary-reference set, usually 1-3 examples; stop when the target decision is supported. Original creator pages, accessible Mobbin examples, GSAP Showcase, Figma Community, or relevant Awwwards examples may help. A landing page/search snippet does not prove the example was inspected.

For each used reference, retain its direct URL, provenance, what was seen, intended adaptation, and access/reuse limits. Verify rights for assets/fonts actually proposed for reuse and Thai/Latin glyph coverage. Do not buy subscriptions, bypass access blocks, or imply unseen interactions were tested.

External skill installation needs explicit authorization and inspection of its instructions, scripts, license, pinned provenance, dependencies, and permissions. Research is not installation authority.

## 3. Return a scoped recommendation

Prefer one recommended direction. Add alternatives only if they clarify a real unresolved choice or the owner requests them. Do not expand the named page/section into homepage and room-page deliverables unless requested.

Describe only what implementation needs: hierarchy, Thai-English typography, palette, layout/spacing, image/content mapping, relevant desktop/mobile states, and purposeful motion with reduced-motion fallback. Keep it specific to built-in work, materials, and craft. New dependencies/systems follow AGENTS scope boundaries.

The executor may settle routine design details and perform affected responsive, interaction, accessibility, asset, and motion self-checks. No separate Reviewer or Designer sign-off is required before delivery/publication.

## Handoff

Use a compact English record and concise Thai explanation through Manager:

```text
Target / requested outcome / owner direction:
Recommendation / necessary implementation detail:
References / facts / content assumptions / rights or access limits:
Relevant checks / known tradeoffs / next executor action:
```

A visual comparison with an approved reference is optional task support when requested, not a release gate. Report only observed findings; distinguish proposals, installed files, and verified runtime/tool capabilities.
